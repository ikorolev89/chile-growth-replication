# Third-party material

`code/stm/R_functions.R` is the authors' adapted version of code shared by Peter Dreuw, as acknowledged in the paper and supplement. Relevant reference: Dreuw, Peter (2023), “Structural time series models and synthetic controls—assessing the impact of the euro adoption,” *Empirical Economics* 64(2), 681–725.

No explicit redistribution license for that shared helper was present in the supplied files. The [original article's code-availability statement](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00181-022-02257-x) says the analysis code is available upon request; its open-access article license does not by itself establish the terms of separately shared code. This archive retains the helper's existing comments and attribution. No MIT or other blanket license is assigned to this helper, and its inclusion does not grant additional rights beyond those of the original rights holder.

R packages are restored from their own repositories and retain their own licenses. In particular, augsynth and fect identify MIT licensing, CausalImpact identifies Apache 2.0 licensing, and the remaining packages' terms are available in their package metadata. The archive does not vendor installed R package libraries.

World Bank and Maddison data attribution and licensing are described separately in `DATA_SOURCES.md`. The publisher's article PDF is not included.
